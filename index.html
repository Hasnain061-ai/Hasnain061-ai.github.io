Heatwave Intelligence

A portal for NDMA Pakistan

Shows how heat in Pakistan has changed since 1980, and which districts face a heatwave in the next seven days.

Built with Mapbox GL JS, plain JavaScript, a FastAPI backend and Python climate tooling.

  • 45 years of station data on one mapPMD records from 1980 to 2024 on a 0.45° grid (about 50 km), with five heatwave measures at four percentile thresholds.
  • Compare periods and yearsA swipe, side-by-side or difference view between any two periods or years.
  • Trends, rankings and recordsA Mann-Kendall trend test with Sen's slope, national and station rankings, all-time records, and a chart for any place.
  • 7-day heatwave outlookThe ECMWF forecast, bias-corrected to 111 PMD stations. A district goes to Alert or Severe when the forecast stays 4.5 °C or 6.5 °C above that date's normal for two days or more.
  • A chat assistant that answers with dataQuestions in English or Roman Urdu. Every number it gives comes from a data tool, and it draws its answer on the map.

Checked against the record. Tested on July and August 2026 against 111 PMD stations, the outlook caught 80% of observed heatwave days, and 23% of its calls were false alarms. The portal prints this beside the forecast, along with the months it has not been tested on yet.
