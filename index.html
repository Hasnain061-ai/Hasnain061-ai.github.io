PORTAL 01

Heatwave Intelligence

A portal for NDMA Pakistan

Shows how heat in Pakistan has changed since 1980, and which districts face a heatwave in the next seven days.

Built with Mapbox GL JS, plain JavaScript, a FastAPI backend and Python climate tooling.

Watch the demo video on LinkedIn Screens and details on GitHub
  • 0145 years of station data on one mapPMD records from 1980 to 2024 on a 0.45° grid (about 50 km), with five heatwave measures at four percentile thresholds.
  • 02Compare periods and yearsA swipe, side-by-side or difference view between any two periods or years.
  • 03Trends, rankings and recordsA Mann-Kendall trend test with Sen's slope, national and station rankings, all-time records, and a chart for any place.
  • 047-day heatwave outlookThe ECMWF forecast, bias-corrected to 111 PMD stations. A district goes to Alert or Severe when the forecast stays 4.5 °C or 6.5 °C above that date's normal for two days or more.
  • 05A chat assistant that answers with dataQuestions in English or Roman Urdu. Every number it gives comes from a data tool, and it draws its answer on the map.

Checked against the record. Tested on July and August 2026 against 111 PMD stations, the outlook caught 80% of observed heatwave days, and 23% of its calls were false alarms. The portal prints this beside the forecast, along with the months it has not been tested on yet.

PORTAL 02

Ocean Intelligence

A portal for NDMA Pakistan

Shows how acidity, heat and oxygen in Pakistan's seas have changed since the 1980s, and where they are heading by 2050.

Built with Mapbox GL JS, plain JavaScript, Chart.js and a Python analysis pipeline.

Watch the demo video on LinkedIn Screens and details on GitHub
  • 01Acidification, mappedSurface pH in Pakistan's Exclusive Economic Zone fell from 8.08 in 1985 to 8.02 in 2024, about 16% more acidity, and it fell in every area checked.
  • 02Warming and marine heatwavesThe sea has warmed by 1.0 to 1.3 °C since 1982. Marine heatwave days rose from about 23 a year (1991–2020 average) to about 100 (2021–2025).
  • 03Low oxygen on the shelfIn the southwest monsoon, 24% of the Sindh shelf floor sits below 2 mg/L of oxygen.
  • 04Outlook to 2050Low- and high-emission paths, side by side or against today.
  • 05A chat assistantQuestions in English or Roman Urdu, answered from the study's numbers.
PORTAL 03

Heatwave and Flood Digital Twin

In development · prototype

The next step after Heatwave Intelligence: a 3D digital twin of a real neighbourhood with two hazard modules, heatwave and flood, that shows what is exposed to each and how vulnerable it is, building by building.

Built with CesiumJS on real 3D geography. Modules: heatwave and flood, both in the same twin.

  • 01Heatwave and flood in one twinBoth modules run on the same 3D neighbourhood and are switched from one scenario panel.
  • 02Exposure and vulnerabilityWhich buildings and roads sit in harm's way, and how vulnerable they are, mapped down to a single building.
  • 03A real neighbourhood in 3DIslamabad's F-8/1 sector, with 476 mapped building footprints.
  • 04Scenarios through timeAn event timeline hour by hour, before and during the event, in natural or analytical view.
  • 05A guided storyA six-chapter tour from the whole neighbourhood down to one street and one building.

Work in progress. The flood scenario in these screens is synthetic, used to build and test the twin. Both modules are still being built.
